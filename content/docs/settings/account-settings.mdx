title: Account settings
description: Update your personal information, appearance preferences, and notification delivery.

Account settings control how Clave looks and feels for you personally. Changes here only affect your experience — they don't change anything for other team members.

## Accessing account settings

Open **Settings** from the left sidebar (or press **G then S**). Under the **Personal** section, click **Account**.

## Profile information

### Profile photo

Your profile photo appears throughout Clave — in issue assignee lists, comments, the notification feed, and team member lists.

To change your photo:
1. Click **Change photo**.
2. Select an image file (any image format, up to 2 MB).
3. Adjust the crop area and confirm.

The photo is uploaded and saved automatically after cropping.

If you haven't uploaded a photo yet, you'll see a prompt in a warning bar at the top of the Account settings page — uploading a photo helps teammates recognize you.

### Full name

Your display name across Clave. Click the field, edit your name, then click outside to save. The name must be at least 2 characters.

### Email address

Your sign-in address. This is set by your Google account and shown here for reference. It cannot be edited from within Clave.

### Job title

An optional label that appears on your profile, for example "Designer" or "Engineer". Click the field, edit it, then click outside to save. Maximum 100 characters.

## Appearance

### Theme

Choose how Clave looks:

- **System default** — automatically matches your operating system's light or dark mode setting.
- **Light** — always use the light theme.
- **Dark** — always use the dark theme.

Changes take effect immediately.

### Compact mode

Toggle compact mode on to use a denser layout with less spacing throughout the app. Useful on smaller screens or if you prefer more content visible at once.

### Sidebar collapsed

When enabled, Clave starts with the left sidebar collapsed every time you load the app. The sidebar can still be expanded by clicking the sidebar trigger or pressing **Cmd+B**.

## Location and time

### Timezone

Your timezone is used to display dates and times correctly throughout Clave — due dates, notification timestamps, and scheduled reminders all respect this setting.

Select your timezone from the dropdown. The list uses IANA timezone names (for example, "America/New_York"). Changes save immediately.

## Authentication

### User ID

A read-only identifier for your account. Click **Copy** if you need to share it with support. This ID is unique to your user account across all workspaces.
