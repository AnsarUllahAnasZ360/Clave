Billing is managed at the organization level. Workspace admins can view the current plan, see usage against plan limits, and upgrade or change the subscription.

## Accessing billing

Open **Settings** from the sidebar. In the left navigation, click **Billing** (located in the workspace settings section).

Billing settings require admin access. Non-admin members see a locked view.

## Available plans

Clave offers three plans:

### Free

**$0 — forever**

The Free plan gives you the core Clave experience with the following limits:

- A set number of workspace members
- A set number of workspaces
- A set amount of file storage
- A monthly AI message quota

Free is ideal for small teams evaluating Clave or working on early-stage projects.

### Pro

**$12 per member per month**

The Pro plan unlocks the full Clave feature set including:

- More workspace members and workspaces
- Higher file storage quota
- Higher monthly AI message quota
- Projects, documents, and whiteboards

### Enterprise

**Custom pricing**

The Enterprise plan includes everything in Pro plus priority support, SSO, audit log, dedicated support, and custom integrations. Contact us to discuss pricing.

## Viewing your current plan

The billing page shows your organization's active plan in the header. The plan badge indicates whether you're on Free, Pro, or Enterprise, and the subscription status badge shows whether the subscription is **Active**, **Trialing**, or **Past due**.

## Viewing usage

Below the plan cards, the **usage summary** shows how much of each plan limit you're currently using:

- **Members** — current active member count vs. your plan's maximum
- **Workspaces** — current workspace count vs. your plan's maximum
- **Storage** — used file storage vs. your plan's limit
- **AI messages** — AI messages used this billing period vs. your monthly limit

Usage bars update in real-time as your team grows.

## Upgrading your plan

To upgrade from Free to Pro:
1. Click **Upgrade** on the Pro plan card.
2. You'll be taken to a secure Stripe checkout page.
3. Enter your payment details and complete the purchase.
4. Your organization's plan is upgraded immediately after payment.

## Managing your subscription

If you're already on a paid plan, click **Manage subscription** to open the Stripe customer portal. From the portal you can:

- Update your payment method
- Download invoices and receipts
- Cancel your subscription
- View billing history

Changes made in the Stripe portal take effect on your next billing cycle.

## Plan limit warnings

If your team approaches or exceeds a plan limit, Clave displays a warning prompt. When a hard limit is hit (for example, the AI message cap), the relevant feature pauses until the next billing period or until you upgrade.
