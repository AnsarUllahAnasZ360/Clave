---
title: Comments and Threads
description: Add inline comments and threaded discussions to any document.
---

Comments let you annotate specific passages in a document and have threaded discussions without editing the document text itself.

## Open the comments sidebar

Click the **comment bubble icon** (💬) in the document header to open the Comments sidebar. The sidebar slides in from the right and shows all comment threads for the document.

You can also click **Add comment** in the button row below the document title (visible when hovering over the hero area).

## Add a comment

1. **Select text** in the document body that you want to comment on.
2. A floating toolbar appears above your selection. Click the **comment icon** in the toolbar.
3. The comment input appears. Type your comment.
4. Press **Enter** or click **Submit** to post.

The selected text becomes highlighted, and the comment appears in the sidebar.

![Adding a comment to selected text](/docs/screenshots/documents-add-comment.png)

## Comment threads

Each comment starts a thread. A thread can have multiple replies from any workspace member.

**To reply to a thread:**
1. Click the thread in the sidebar to open the thread detail view.
2. Type your reply in the input field at the bottom.
3. Press **Enter** to post.

## @mentioning teammates

In any comment or reply, type `@` followed by a teammate's name to mention them. They'll receive a notification in their Inbox.

## Edit or delete a comment

Hover over your own comment to reveal the edit (pencil) and delete (trash) icons.

- **Edit** — Opens the comment for editing. Save with **Enter**.
- **Delete** — Removes the comment. Deleted comments cannot be restored.

## Resolve a thread

When a comment thread has been addressed, you can mark it resolved:

1. Open the thread in the sidebar.
2. Click the **Resolve** button (checkmark icon).

Resolved threads are shown with reduced opacity in the sidebar. They remain visible so you have a record of past discussions.

**To reopen a resolved thread**, click the thread and click **Unresolve**.

## Thread sorting

In the comments sidebar, threads are sorted with **unresolved threads first**, then by creation time (newest first). This keeps active discussions at the top.

## Comments notifications

When a teammate @mentions you in a document comment, you receive a notification in your Inbox. You can click the notification to jump directly to the document and the relevant thread.
