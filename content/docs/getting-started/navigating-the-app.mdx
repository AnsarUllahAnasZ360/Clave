## The sidebar

The left sidebar is your main navigation in Clave. It is always visible while you are inside a workspace.

![App sidebar](/docs/screenshots/app-sidebar.png)

### Workspace selector

At the very top of the sidebar is the **workspace selector**. Click it to:

- Switch between workspaces within the current organization.
- Switch to a different organization.
- Create or join a new workspace.

### Main navigation

Below the workspace selector, the sidebar lists the primary areas of your workspace:

| Section | What it contains |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Chat** | AI chat threads for your workspace |
| **Inbox** | All notifications and @mentions |
| **My issues** | Issues assigned to you |
| **Projects** | All projects in the workspace |
| **Docs** | Documents and rich-text files |
| **Boards** | Whiteboards and diagrams |
| **Clients** | CRM contacts and client records |
| **Performance** | Analytics and workspace metrics |

A badge on **Inbox** shows your unread notification count.

### Recents

The **Recents** section tracks the last items you visited — issues, documents, projects, boards, and chat threads. Click any item to jump back to it.

Click the **Recents** label to collapse or expand this section.

### Favorites

Items you have starred appear in the **Favorites** section. You can star projects, documents, whiteboards, and clients. Favorites persist across sessions so your most-used items are always one click away.

### Active Projects

The **Active Projects** section lists projects that are currently in progress. Click a project to open its dashboard directly from the sidebar.

### User menu

At the bottom of the sidebar, click your avatar or name to open the user menu. From here you can:

- Access account settings.
- Go to workspace settings.
- Sign out.

## Collapse the sidebar

Click the collapse toggle (the arrow at the edge of the sidebar) to collapse it to icon-only mode. In icon-only mode, hover over any icon to see a tooltip with the section name.

Click the toggle again to expand the sidebar.

## Search

The **Search** bar at the top of the sidebar (below the workspace selector) lets you quickly find items across the workspace. Click it or press **Cmd+K** to open the command palette.

## Command palette

The command palette is the fastest way to navigate and take action in Clave. Open it with **Cmd+K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows/Linux).

From the command palette you can:

- Jump to any project, document, issue, or board.
- Create a new issue, document, or board.
- Run AI actions.
- Access settings.

Start typing to filter results. Use arrow keys to select and **Enter** to navigate.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Action | Shortcut |
|--------|----------|
| Open command palette | **Cmd+K** |
| Open AI chat | **Cmd+J** |
| Search | **Cmd+K** |

More keyboard shortcuts are available in [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/settings/keyboard-shortcuts).

## Switching organizations

To switch to a different organization, click the workspace selector at the top of the sidebar and choose **Switch organization**. You are taken to the **Organizations** page where you can click any org to enter it.
