## What is Clave?

Clave is a thinking workspace where your team and AI work together. It combines issue tracking, rich documents, real-time whiteboards, and a deeply integrated AI layer in one place.

Whether you are managing a sprint, writing a spec, or asking the AI to triage a backlog, Clave keeps everything connected.

## What you can do in Clave

- **Track work** — Create projects, manage issues across kanban boards, lists, and timelines. Organize work into sprints and milestones.
- **Write together** — Draft documents with a rich editor, collaborate in real time with teammates, and leave threaded comments.
- **Draw and diagram** — Use whiteboards for brainstorming, system diagrams, and anything visual. Share boards publicly with a link.
- **Manage clients** — Keep a lightweight CRM alongside your work. Link client contacts to projects.
- **Talk to your AI** — Open the AI chat to ask questions, create issues, search your workspace, and get things done using natural language.
- **Stay informed** — The unified inbox collects all notifications and @mentions. The AI can summarize what you missed.

## How Clave is organized

Clave uses a two-level hierarchy:

- **Organizations** are the top-level container. A company, team, or personal account lives in one organization.
- **Workspaces** live inside organizations. Each workspace has its own projects, issues, docs, boards, and settings.

You can belong to multiple organizations and switch between them from the sidebar.
