The Inbox collects every notification that matters to you in a single, focused view. Instead of checking individual projects or issues, you come here first to see what needs your attention.

## Accessing the inbox

Click **Inbox** in the left sidebar, or press **G then I** anywhere in the app to jump straight to it.

![Inbox overview](/docs/screenshots/inbox-overview.png)

## Notification types

Clave sends notifications for the following events:

| Type | What triggers it |
|------|-----------------|
| **Issues** | You're assigned to an issue, a status changes on an issue you follow |
| **Mentions** | Someone @mentions you in a comment or description |
| **Comments** | A new comment is posted on an issue or document you're involved with |
| **Projects** | A project update is posted for a project you're a member of |
| **Documents** | A document you have access to is shared or commented on |
| **Whiteboards** | A whiteboard is updated |
| **Reminders** | Due date approaching, overdue, or stale issue alerts |

## The inbox layout

The inbox is split into two panels.

**Left panel** — a scrollable list of your notifications. Each item shows the actor's avatar, what they did, the relevant item title, and how long ago it happened. Unread notifications have a small dot on the left edge.

**Right panel** — a preview of the selected notification. For issue notifications you can update status, priority, assignee, and project directly from this panel without navigating away.

## Reading notifications

Click any notification in the list to select it. Clave automatically marks it as read when you open it.

To manually toggle read/unread on the selected notification, press **U**.

To mark all notifications as read at once, click **Mark all read** in the header, or press **Alt+U**.

## Filtering notifications

Click the **Filter** button (next to the Inbox tab) to narrow down what you see:

- **Type** — choose one or more: Issues, Comments, Mentions, Projects, Documents, Whiteboards, Reminders
- **Status** — All notifications, Unread only, or Read only

Filters are applied immediately. Click **Apply** to confirm or **Clear** to remove all filters.

You can also search notifications using the search bar in the top-right of the inbox. Press **Cmd+F** to focus it.

## Snoozed notifications

The **Snoozed** tab shows notifications you've deferred. Switch to it by clicking the tab or pressing the snooze button on any notification.

To snooze a notification, hover over it and click the clock icon. Choose a preset:

- 1 hour
- 3 hours
- Tomorrow morning (9:00 AM)
- Next Monday morning (9:00 AM)
- Custom date and time

Snoozed notifications disappear from the main inbox until the snooze time arrives, then reappear automatically. Press **H** as a shortcut to snooze the selected notification for 1 hour.

## Archiving and deleting

**Archive** — moves the notification out of the active inbox without deleting it. Press **E** to archive the selected notification.

**Delete** — permanently removes the notification. Press **Backspace** or **Delete** to delete the selected notification.

To delete all visible notifications at once, press **Shift+Backspace**.

To delete all read notifications, press **Cmd+D** (or **Ctrl+D** on Windows/Linux).

## AI smart digest

At the top of the inbox you'll see the **AI digest card**. This is a short AI-generated summary of your unread notifications — what changed, what needs attention, and what can wait. The digest refreshes each time you open the inbox with new unread items.

## Inbox keyboard shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|
| J / ↓ | Move selection down |
| K / ↑ | Move selection up |
| U | Toggle read/unread |
| H | Snooze 1 hour |
| E | Archive |
| Backspace | Delete selected |
| Shift+Backspace | Delete all visible |
| Cmd+D | Delete all read |
| Alt+U | Mark all read |
| Cmd+F | Focus search |

Issue shortcuts (S, A, P, L) also work in the inbox when an issue notification is selected — they update the issue without leaving the inbox.
