title: Notification settings

Notification settings let you control where Clave sends your updates. You can configure delivery channels from the **Settings** page under **Personal → Notifications**.

## Accessing notification settings

1. Open Settings from the sidebar (or press **G then S**).
2. Under the **Personal** section, click **Notifications**.

## Notification channels

Clave supports two notification channels, each controlled by an independent toggle:

### In-app notifications

When enabled, notifications flow into your Inbox inside Clave. This is on by default.

Turn this off only if you want to stop receiving in-app notifications entirely. Note that you will still have access to the Inbox page, but it will stop updating.

### Email notifications

When enabled, Clave sends email summaries to the address on your account for important events. This is on by default.

Your email address is shown in **Settings → Account** and is set when you sign up with Google. It cannot be changed from within Clave.

## Enabling and disabling channels

Click the toggle next to each channel to turn it on or off. Changes save immediately — there is no Save button.

When you disable a channel, a confirmation toast appears confirming the change was saved.

## Tips for managing notification volume

- If your inbox gets noisy, use the **Filter** button in the inbox to show only specific notification types rather than disabling in-app notifications entirely.
- Snooze notifications you're not ready for rather than deleting them — they'll come back at the right time.
- Use the AI smart digest in the inbox to quickly catch up without reading each notification individually.

For inbox-specific controls like filtering, snoozing, and bulk deletion, see the [Inbox guide](/docs/inbox-and-notifications/inbox).
