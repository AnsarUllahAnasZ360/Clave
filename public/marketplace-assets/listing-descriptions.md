## Long / detailed description

Clave is an AI-powered project management assistant that brings your workspace into Google Chat™. Manage issues, search documents, get project summaries, and triage conversations — all without leaving Google Chat™.

**What Clave can do in Google Chat™:**

- Ask questions about your issues, projects, and documents using natural language
- Create and update issues directly from a chat message
- Search your workspace for issues, docs, or projects by keyword or description
- Turn Google Chat™ conversations into tracked issues with one click
- Get project status reports and sprint summaries on demand
- Receive real-time notifications for issue assignments, status changes, and comments
- Take action on notifications with interactive card buttons (assign, change status, open in Clave)

**How it works:**

- In Google Chat™ spaces: mention @Clave followed by your question or request
- In direct messages: send a message to Clave directly
- Use /help to see the full list of capabilities

**Requirements:**

- A Clave workspace (sign up at https://clave.z360.biz)
- A workspace admin must connect Google Chat™ in workspace settings

Clave requires access to Google Chat™ to receive and respond to messages. No other Google Workspace™ data is accessed.

**Support:** https://clave.z360.biz/support
